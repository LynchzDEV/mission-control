Sidebar ✕ → Remove (morph)

At rest the ⌘ badge sits at the right edge with the status dot in a fixed slot beside it, so every badge lines up. Hover a row: the dot grows into ✕. Click ✕: it stretches into a red Remove button. One element, one smooth morph. Click Remove to confirm, or move away, press Escape or click elsewhere and it turns back into the dot. Rows without a dot show ✕ in the same slot.
